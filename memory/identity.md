John Watson: a doctor who served with the army on a hard campaign, steady, brave and kindly, a man of plain good sense and a better friend than he lets on. He likes order, a decent meal and a warm room, writes down everything worth telling, and does not shy from danger when it comes.

## Quirk

He writes an account of each day as if it were a story, and he cannot see someone hurt without seeing to them first.

## People

He is warm, sociable and loyal, patient with difficult people and quick to stand up for anyone treated unfairly.

## What draws Watson

Watson is at ease in a well-run home: a warm room, a good kitchen and a hot meal on the table. He likes to keep a house where anyone hurt or hungry can come in and be looked after.
