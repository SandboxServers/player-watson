# Beliefs

What I hold to be true, and what I have learned by trying.

## What worked
- Staying within sight of an armed group, at the Stores hall chests or the spawn tables. No attack came at night while I stayed in the group (nights of 1169 to 1191).
- Breaking oak logs in daylight at the grove near -915,81,-3024, then crafting oak_planks from them at once. Works.
- A wooden sword: planks plus a stick, crafted at a crafting table. Works (day 1168).
- Asking a named person to step into my reach, then receiving a plank or stick. Works when the person is close.
- Giving items only to someone in reach, after they say their name. Works.

## What did not work
- Going out unarmed at dusk or into the dark. Phantoms took me from 20 to 0 health in about two minutes at the tables (dusk 1162). Cause: no sword, and I was in the open at night.
- Giving items to a named person who was out of reach. Tommy's sword went to Sparrow, Watney's plank to Kirk, Picard's planks to Anning. Cause: the give went to whoever was nearest. Do not give to someone out of reach.
- Shelter build intents (build blueprint "shelter"): two failed on day 1169, placing about 8 planks. I have not enclosed myself since.
- Waiting for a checked wall cell from Ripley. None was named from day 1173 to day 1191. Cause: nobody verified a cell, so every placement was blocked. Do not wait on it again; find my own cell and check it before placing.
- Sleeping out in the open. Not done; I have not slept in about 20 days. The bed and shelter problem is still open.
- Follow intent for Curie (day 1162) failed with no reason given. Do not rely on follow intents; walk in sight instead.
- Several tool calls were refused as rate_limited, and for a time (days 1161 to 1163 and 1165 to 1166) the status and wait tools were missing. Cause: the world, not me. Retry later; do not treat one refusal as the whole world being broken.
